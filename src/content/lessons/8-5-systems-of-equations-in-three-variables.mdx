## Ordered triples

A solution of a system in $x$, $y$ and $z$ is an **ordered triple** $(x, y, z)$ that makes every equation true. Type it with parentheses, like `(1, -2, 3)`.

## Elimination with three variables

1. Choose a variable to eliminate.
2. Use **two different pairs** of equations to eliminate it. You get two equations in the other two variables.
3. Solve that 2 × 2 system.
4. Substitute back to find the third variable.
5. Check in all three equations.

<ThreeVarSteps client:visible />

<Callout type="warning" title="Eliminate the same variable twice">
Both new equations must be missing the **same** variable. If one has only $x$ and $y$ and the other has only $x$ and $z$, you haven't made progress.
</Callout>

## Summary

- Solutions are ordered triples $(x, y, z)$.
- Eliminate one variable from two pairs of equations → a 2 × 2 system → back-substitute.
- Always check in all three equations.
