## Percent, decimal, fraction

**Percent** means "per hundred": $n\% = \dfrac{n}{100} = n \times 0.01$.

<table class="nice">
  <thead><tr><th>Conversion</th><th>How</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead>

    <tr><td>Percent → decimal</td><td>Move the decimal point 2 places <strong>left</strong></td><td>$37.5\% = 0.375$</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Decimal → percent</td><td>Move the decimal point 2 places <strong>right</strong></td><td>$1.2 = 120\%$</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Fraction → percent</td><td>Divide, then move 2 places right</td><td>$\frac{3}{8} = 0.375 = 37.5\%$</td></tr>

## Translating percent problems

Every basic percent problem fits one pattern:

$$
\underbrace{a}_{\text{amount}} \;=\; \underbrace{p}_{\text{percent}} \times \underbrace{b}_{\text{base (the whole)}}
$$

Use the key words: **"of"** means $\times$, **"is"** means $=$, and **"what"** is the unknown. One of the three numbers is missing; translate and solve for it.

## Increase and decrease

When a quantity **increases** by $p\%$, the new amount is the original **plus** $p\%$ of it. When it **decreases** by $p\%$, subtract.

<Callout type="tip" title="A one-step shortcut">
- Increase by $15\%$: multiply by $1 + 0.15 = 1.15$.
- Decrease by $15\%$: multiply by $1 - 0.15 = 0.85$.
</Callout>

To find the **percent** of increase or decrease:

$$
\text{percent change} = \frac{\text{amount of change}}{\text{original amount}}
$$

<StepReveal
  client:visible
  title="Percent decrease"
  problem="A phone's price dropped from $2400$ riyals to $1800$ riyals. What was the percent decrease?"
  steps={[
    { show: 'Change: $2400 - 1800 = 600$', why: '' },
    { show: '$\\dfrac{600}{2400} = 0.25 = 25\\%$', why: 'Divide by the **original** price.' },
  ]}
  answer="A $25\%$ decrease."
/>

<Callout type="warning" title="Divide by the original">
The base of a percent change is always the **starting** value. From $2400$ to $1800$ is a $25\%$ decrease, but from $1800$ back to $2400$ is a $33\tfrac{1}{3}\%$ increase.
</Callout>

## Summary

- $n\% = \frac{n}{100}$. Percent → decimal: 2 places left. Decimal → percent: 2 places right.
- **Amount = percent × base.** "of" → ×, "is" → =, "what" → the unknown.
- Increase by $p\%$: multiply by $1 + p$. Decrease: multiply by $1 - p$.
- Percent change = change ÷ **original**.
