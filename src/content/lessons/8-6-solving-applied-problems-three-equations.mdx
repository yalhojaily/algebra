## Triangle angles

The angles of a triangle add up to $180°$ — that's always one of the equations.

<StepReveal

  steps={[

## Investments

With simple interest, $I = Pr$. Multiplying the interest equation by $100$ clears the decimals: $0.03x + 0.04y + 0.05z = 410$ becomes $3x + 4y + 5z = 41000$.

## Summary

- Write one equation per fact; look for equations with only two variables — they make substitution easy.
- Solve, then check every fact in the original problem.
