## FOIL

To multiply two binomials, multiply the **F**irst terms, the **O**utside terms, the **I**nside terms and the **L**ast terms. It's just the four products of 4.5 in a fixed order:

$$(A + B)(C + D) = \underbrace{AC}_{F} + \underbrace{AD}_{O} + \underbrace{BC}_{I} + \underbrace{BD}_{L}$$

## The square of a binomial

$(A + B)^2 = (A + B)(A + B)$. FOIL gives $A^2 + AB + AB + B^2$ — the middle product appears **twice**. The picture shows it: a square of side $A + B$ is made of an $A^2$ square, a $B^2$ square and **two** $AB$ rectangles.

<SquareArea client:visible />

<Callout type="tip" title="Squares of binomials">
$$(A + B)^2 = A^2 + 2AB + B^2 \qquad\qquad (A - B)^2 = A^2 - 2AB + B^2$$

Square the first term, **double the product**, square the last term.
</Callout>

    { show: '$= 9x^2 - 30x + 25$', why: '' },

/>

## A sum times a difference

When the two binomials have the same terms, one with $+$ and one with $-$, the outside and inside products cancel:

$$(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - AB + AB - B^2 = A^2 - B^2$$

<Callout type="tip" title="Product of a sum and a difference">
$$(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - B^2$$

The answer is a **difference of squares** — there is no middle term.
</Callout>

    { show: '$(4y)^2 - 3^2 = 16y^2 - 9$', why: '' },

  ]}
/>

## Common mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="(A + B)² ≠ A² + B²">
This is the most common error in algebra. $(x + 5)^2 = x^2 + 10x + 25$ — check with $x = 1$: $36 \ne 26$.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Forgetting to double">
The middle term of $(3x - 5)^2$ is $-2(3x)(5) = -30x$, not $-15x$.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Squaring only part of a term">
$(3x)^2 = 9x^2$, not $3x^2$. Use parentheses when you substitute $A = 3x$.
</Callout>

## Summary

- **FOIL:** $(A + B)(C + D) = AC + AD + BC + BD$.
- **Square:** $(A \pm B)^2 = A^2 \pm 2AB + B^2$.
- **Sum × difference:** $(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - B^2$.
- Look for the pattern first; if none fits, FOIL always works.
