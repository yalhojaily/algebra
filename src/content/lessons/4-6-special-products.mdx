## FOIL

To multiply two binomials, multiply the **F**irst terms, the **O**uter terms, the **I**nner terms, and the **L**ast terms:

$$(A + B)(C + D) = \underbrace{AC}_{F} + \underbrace{AD}_{O} + \underbrace{BC}_{I} + \underbrace{BD}_{L}$$

## Squares of binomials and sums times differences

<SquareArea client:visible />

<Callout type="tip" title="Two patterns to know by heart">
$$(A + B)^2 = A^2 + 2AB + B^2 \qquad (A - B)^2 = A^2 - 2AB + B^2$$
$$(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - B^2$$
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="(A + B)² is not A² + B²">
$(x + 3)^2 = x^2 + 6x + 9$. Check with a number: if $x = 1$, then $(1 + 3)^2 = 16$, but $1^2 + 3^2 = 10$. The middle term $2AB$ makes up the difference.
</Callout>

## Summary

- **FOIL** for any two binomials.
- $(A \pm B)^2 = A^2 \pm 2AB + B^2$ — three terms, never two.
- $(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - B^2$.
