## Intersection and union

- The **intersection** $A \cap B$ is the set of elements in **both** $A$ and $B$.
- The **union** $A \cup B$ is the set of elements in $A$, in $B$, or in both.
- The **empty set** $\varnothing$ has no elements.

$\{1, 2, 3, 4\} \cap \{3, 4, 5\} = \{3, 4\}$, and $\{1, 2, 3, 4\} \cup \{3, 4, 5\} = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$.

## Conjunctions

A **conjunction** joins two inequalities with **and**. A double inequality like $-3 < 2x + 1 \le 7$ is a conjunction: you can solve it by doing the same thing to **all three parts**.

## Disjunctions

A **disjunction** joins two inequalities with **or**. Solve each one separately and take the **union**.

## Summary

- **And** → intersection (overlap). **Or** → union (everything shaded).
- Solve a double inequality by working on all three parts.
- An "and" with no overlap has the empty set $\varnothing$ as its solution.
