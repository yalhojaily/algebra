A rectangle has area $x^2 + 7x + 10$ and width $x + 2$. How long is it? You need $(x^2 + 7x + 10) \div (x + 2)$. Polynomial division undoes multiplication — and it's the tool you'll use to check factoring in Chapter 5.

## Dividing by a monomial

Divide **each term** of the polynomial by the monomial:

$$\frac{a + b}{c} = \frac{a}{c} + \frac{b}{c}$$

## Long division by a binomial

The cycle is the same as long division with numbers:

1. **Divide** the first term of what's left by the first term of the divisor.
2. **Multiply** that result by the whole divisor.
3. **Subtract** (change all the signs!).
4. **Bring down** and repeat until the remainder has lower degree than the divisor.

<LongDivision client:visible />

<Callout type="warning" title="Subtract every term">
After multiplying, you subtract the **whole** product. Writing the product with its signs changed, then adding, prevents sign mistakes.
</Callout>

## Remainders and missing terms

If the remainder isn't $0$, write the answer as $\;\text{quotient} + \dfrac{\text{remainder}}{\text{divisor}}$.

If a power of $x$ is missing in the dividend, write it with a $0$ coefficient so the columns line up: $x^3 - 8 = x^3 + 0x^2 + 0x - 8$.

## Summary

- By a monomial: divide each term.
- By a binomial: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — repeat.
- Answer with remainder: $Q + \frac{R}{\text{divisor}}$. Check: divisor × quotient + remainder = dividend.
