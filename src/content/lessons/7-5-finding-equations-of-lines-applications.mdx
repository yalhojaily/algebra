## Point–slope form

The line with slope $m$ through the point $(x_1, y_1)$ is

$$y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$$

This comes straight from the slope formula: $\dfrac{y - y_1}{x - x_1} = m$.

## Summary

- Point–slope: $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$; solve for $y$ to get $y = mx + b$.
- Two points: slope first, then point–slope.
- Parallel: same slope. Perpendicular: opposite reciprocal.
- Models: slope = rate of change; use the line to predict.
