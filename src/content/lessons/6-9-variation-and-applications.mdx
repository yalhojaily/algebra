<Callout type="why">
Your pay grows with the hours you work. A trip takes less time when you drive faster. These two everyday patterns — **direct** and **inverse** variation — are described by the simplest rational equations.
</Callout>

## Direct and inverse variation

- $y$ **varies directly** as $x$ if $y = kx$ for some positive constant $k$. Then $\frac{y}{x} = k$ stays the same.
- $y$ **varies inversely** as $x$ if $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$. Then $xy = k$ stays the same.

$k$ is called the **constant of variation**.

## Applications

1. Decide: direct ($y = kx$) or inverse ($y = \frac{k}{x}$)?
2. Use the given pair of values to find $k$.
3. Use the equation to answer the question.

<Callout type="tip" title="Sense check">
Direct: more of one means **more** of the other. Inverse: more of one means **less** of the other. If 4 workers need 6 hours, 8 workers should need **fewer** hours — 3, not 12.
</Callout>

## Summary

- Direct: $y = kx$, $\frac{y}{x} = k$; the graph is a line through the origin.
- Inverse: $y = \frac{k}{x}$, $xy = k$; as $x$ increases, $y$ decreases.
- Find $k$ from one pair of values, then use the equation.
