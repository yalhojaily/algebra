## Adding polynomials

To add polynomials, remove the parentheses and **collect like terms**. Writing them in columns, one column per power of $x$, keeps like terms lined up.

## Opposites and subtraction

The **opposite** of a polynomial changes the sign of **every** term: $-(x^2 - 5x + 3) = -x^2 + 5x - 3$.

To **subtract** a polynomial, add its opposite.

<StepReveal
  client:visible
  title="Subtracting"
  problem="Subtract: $\;(9x^5 + x^3 - 2x) - (-2x^5 + 5x^3 + 6)$"
  steps={[
    { show: '$= 9x^5 + x^3 - 2x + 2x^5 - 5x^3 - 6$', why: 'Change the sign of **every** term in the second polynomial.' },
    { show: '$= 11x^5 - 4x^3 - 2x - 6$', why: 'Collect like terms.' },
  ]}
/>

<Callout type="warning" title="Change every sign">
The most common mistake is changing only the first sign. In $-(-2x^5 + 5x^3 + 6)$, **all three** signs change.
</Callout>

## Summary

- **Add:** collect like terms.
- **Opposite:** change the sign of every term.
- **Subtract:** add the opposite of the second polynomial, then collect like terms.
