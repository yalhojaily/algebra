## Maximum and minimum problems

1. Write the quantity to optimize as a function of one variable.
2. If it is quadratic, find the vertex: $x = -\frac{b}{2a}$.
3. Answer the question asked (the input, the output, or both).

## Fitting a quadratic to data

Three points determine a quadratic $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$. Substituting each point gives three equations in $a$, $b$, $c$ — a system like those in Lesson 8.5.

<Practice client:visible lesson="11.7" skill="fit" compact />

## Summary

- Optimization with a quadratic: find the vertex.
- Three points → three equations → $(a, b, c)$.
