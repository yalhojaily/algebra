A TV's size is its diagonal. A ramp's length depends on its height and run. A GPS computes straight-line distance from coordinates. All three are the Pythagorean theorem — and the answers are usually square roots.

## Right triangles

In a right triangle with legs $a$, $b$ and hypotenuse $c$: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. Give **exact** answers as simplified radicals; type them like `2sqrt(17)` or `sqrt(68)` (both are accepted here).

## Special right triangles

- **45°–45°–90°:** legs $a$ and $a$, hypotenuse $a\sqrt{2}$.
- **30°–60°–90°:** shorter leg $a$, longer leg $a\sqrt{3}$, hypotenuse $2a$.
