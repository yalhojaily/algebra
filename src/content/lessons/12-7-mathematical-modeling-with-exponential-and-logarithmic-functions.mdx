## The exponential model

$$P(t) = P_0 e^{kt}$$

$P_0$ is the amount at $t = 0$. If $k > 0$ it's **exponential growth** with rate $k$; if $k < 0$ it's **exponential decay**.

## Doubling time and half-life

Setting $P(t) = 2P_0$ gives $e^{kT} = 2$, so

$$T = \frac{\ln 2}{k} \qquad\text{(doubling time; for decay, the half-life is } \tfrac{\ln 2}{|k|})$$

## Half-life

A substance with half-life $H$ satisfies $A(t) = A_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/H}$ — the same model, written with base $\frac{1}{2}$.

## Summary

- $P(t) = P_0e^{kt}$: growth for $k > 0$, decay for $k < 0$.
- Doubling time / half-life $= \frac{\ln 2}{|k|}$.
- Use logarithms to solve for $t$ or $k$.
