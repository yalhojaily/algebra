<Callout type="definition" title="Degree with several variables">
The degree of a term is the **sum** of the exponents of its variables: $5x^2y^3$ has degree $5$. The degree of the polynomial is the largest degree of its terms.
</Callout>

## Like terms

Like terms must have **exactly the same variables with the same exponents**: $4x^2y$ and $-x^2y$ are like terms; $x^2y$ and $xy^2$ are not.

## Summary

- Degree of a term = sum of its exponents.
- Like terms: same variables, same exponents.
- Add, subtract, and multiply exactly as with one variable.
