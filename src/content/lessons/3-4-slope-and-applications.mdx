<Callout type="why">
A road sign warns: **8% grade**. A wheelchair ramp may rise at most $1$ cm for every $12$ cm forward. A car's speedometer reads $90$ km/h. All three are **slopes** — the steepness of a line, or how fast one quantity changes compared with another.
</Callout>

## Rise over run

Pick two points on a line. Going from the first to the second, the **rise** is the change in $y$ and the **run** is the change in $x$.

<Callout type="definition" title="Slope">
The **slope** of the line through $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is

$$m = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$
</Callout>

Click the grid to move the two points. The dashed blue segment is the run; the red one is the rise.

## Slope from an equation

In $y = mx + b$, the coefficient $m$ **is** the slope: from the $y$-intercept, each step of $1$ to the right changes $y$ by $m$. If the equation isn't in that form, solve for $y$ first.

<StepReveal
  client:visible
  title="Solve for y, then read m"
  problem="Find the slope of $\;2x - 3y = 9$."
  steps={[
    { show: '$-3y = -2x + 9$', why: 'Subtract $2x$.' },
    { show: '$y = \\tfrac{2}{3}x - 3$', why: 'Divide every term by $-3$.' },
  ]}
  answer="The slope is $\tfrac{2}{3}$."
/>

## Zero and undefined slope

<Callout type="definition" title="Special slopes">
- A **horizontal** line has slope $0$: the rise is $0$, so $m = \frac{0}{\text{run}} = 0$.
- A **vertical** line has **undefined** slope: the run is $0$, and division by $0$ is undefined.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Zero slope is not “no slope”">
Avoid saying "no slope" — it's ambiguous. A horizontal line has slope **zero**. A vertical line's slope is **undefined**.
</Callout>

## Slope as a rate of change

When the axes measure real quantities, slope is a **rate**: (change in the vertical quantity) per (unit change in the horizontal quantity). The **grade** of a road is its slope written as a percent.

<StepReveal
  client:visible
  title="Grade of a road"
  problem="A road rises $12$ m over a horizontal distance of $150$ m. What is its grade?"
  steps={[
    { show: '$m = \\dfrac{12}{150} = 0.08$', why: 'Rise over run.' },
    { show: '$= 8\\%$', why: 'Write as a percent.' },
  ]}
/>

## Summary

- $m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ — rise over run.
- Positive slope rises left to right; negative falls; zero is horizontal; undefined is vertical.
- In $y = mx + b$, the slope is $m$.
- Slope is a rate of change: units of $y$ per unit of $x$.
