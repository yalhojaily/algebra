<Callout type="why">
When is a thrown ball higher than 50 feet? When does a business make a profit? Questions like "when is this expression positive?" are inequalities, and the answer is usually one or more intervals.
</Callout>

## The method

1. Get $0$ on one side and factor.
2. Find the **critical values**: zeros of the numerator, and zeros of the denominator (for rational expressions).
3. They split the number line into intervals. Pick a **test value** in each interval and find the sign.
4. Keep the intervals with the sign you want. Include numerator zeros for $\le$ or $\ge$; **never** include denominator zeros.

## Rational inequalities

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't multiply by the denominator">
Multiplying both sides by $x - 3$ would require knowing whether it's positive or negative. Use the sign chart instead.
</Callout>

<Practice client:visible lesson="11.8" skill="rational" compact />

## Summary

- Critical values split the line; test one value per interval.
- Numerator zeros: included for $\le, \ge$. Denominator zeros: never included.
- Write the answer as a union of intervals.
