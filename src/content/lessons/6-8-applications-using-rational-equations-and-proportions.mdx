If one painter takes 3 hours and another takes 6, how long do they take together? Not 9 hours, and not 4.5 — together they must be faster than either one. Rational equations give the exact answer: 2 hours.

## Work problems

If a job takes $a$ hours, then in one hour you do $\frac{1}{a}$ of it, and in $t$ hours you do $\frac{t}{a}$ of it. Two workers together finish the job when their parts add up to one whole job:

$$\frac{t}{a} + \frac{t}{b} = 1$$

<StepReveal

  title="Work together"
  problem="Ana can paint a fence in 4 hours; Ben takes 12 hours. How long working together?"
  steps={[
    { show: '$\\dfrac{t}{4} + \\dfrac{t}{12} = 1$', why: 'Each does a fraction of the job; together the fractions make $1$.' },
    { show: '$3t + t = 12$', why: 'Multiply by the LCD, $12$.' },
    { show: '$t = 3$ hours', why: 'Check: less than both 4 and 12 ✓' },
  ]}
/>

## Motion problems

Use $\text{time} = \dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$. When two trips take the **same time**, set their times equal.

<StepReveal
  client:visible
  title="Downstream and upstream"
  problem="A boat goes 24 km downstream in the same time it goes 12 km upstream. The current is 4 km/h. Find the boat's speed in still water."
  steps={[
    { show: 'Speed in still water $r$: downstream $r + 4$, upstream $r - 4$', why: 'The current helps one way and slows the other.' },
    { show: '$\\dfrac{24}{r + 4} = \\dfrac{12}{r - 4}$', why: 'The times are equal.' },
    { show: '$24(r - 4) = 12(r + 4) \\;\\Rightarrow\\; 24r - 96 = 12r + 48$', why: 'Cross-multiply.' },
    { show: '$12r = 144,\\quad r = 12$ km/h', why: '' },
  ]}
/>

<QuickCheck
  client:visible
  question="A plane flies $600$ km with a $50$ km/h tailwind. Its speed in still air is $r$. How long does the flight take?"
  options={['$\\dfrac{600}{r + 50}$', '$\\dfrac{600}{r - 50}$', '$600(r + 50)$', '$\\dfrac{r + 50}{600}$']}
  answer={0}
  explain="A tailwind adds to the speed: $r + 50$. Time = distance ÷ speed."
  wrong={{ 1: 'A **tail**wind helps, so the speeds add.', 2: 'Time is distance **divided** by speed.', 3: 'That is speed ÷ distance — upside down.' }}
/>

## Proportions

A **proportion** says two ratios are equal: $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$. Cross-multiplying gives $ad = bc$. Keep the same order in both ratios (cups to cookies on both sides, for example).

<StepReveal
  client:visible
  title="Fuel use"
  problem="A car uses $8$ L of fuel per $100$ km. How much fuel does it need for $350$ km?"
  steps={[
    { show: '$\\dfrac{8}{100} = \\dfrac{x}{350}$', why: 'Liters over kilometers on both sides.' },
    { show: '$100x = 8 \\cdot 350 = 2800$', why: 'Cross-multiply.' },
    { show: '$x = 28$ L', why: 'Check: $350$ km is $3.5$ times $100$ km, and $3.5 \\cdot 8 = 28$ ✓' },
  ]}
/>

<QuickCheck
  client:visible
  question="$3$ cups of flour make $24$ cookies. How many cups are needed for $40$ cookies?"
  options={['$5$', '$320$', '$19$', '$8$']}
  answer={0}
  explain="$\frac{3}{24} = \frac{c}{40} \Rightarrow 24c = 120 \Rightarrow c = 5$."
  wrong={{ 1: 'Keep the same order on both sides: cups over cookies.', 2: 'That adds $16$ to $3$; a proportion multiplies.', 3: '$8$ is the number of cookies per cup.' }}
/>

## Common mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Adding the times">
Two painters taking $3$ h and $6$ h don't need $9$ h together. Add the **rates** $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6}$.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Mixed-up ratios">
$\dfrac{\text{cups}}{\text{cookies}} = \dfrac{\text{cups}}{\text{cookies}}$. Switching the order on one side gives a wrong answer.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Current and wind">
Downstream or with a tailwind, **add** the speeds; upstream or into a headwind, **subtract**.
</Callout>


## Summary

- Work: add the rates, $\frac{t}{a} + \frac{t}{b} = 1$. The combined time is less than either time alone.
- Motion: $t = \frac{d}{r}$; equal times give a rational equation.
- Proportions: same order on both sides, then cross-multiply.
