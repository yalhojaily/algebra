## Subtracting means adding the opposite

Compare these pairs:

$$
8 - 3 = 5 \qquad\qquad 8 + (-3) = 5
$$

$$
10 - 7 = 3 \qquad\qquad 10 + (-7) = 3
$$

In each pair, subtracting a number gives the same result as adding its opposite. This is true for **every** pair of real numbers, so it becomes our definition.

<Callout type="definition" title="Subtraction">
For any real numbers $a$ and $b$:

$$a - b = a + (-b)$$

To subtract, **keep** the first number, **change** subtraction to addition, and **change** the second number to its opposite. Then add.
</Callout>

On the number line, subtracting a positive moves you **left**, and subtracting a negative moves you **right**. Set the second number to a negative and watch the arrow turn around.

<Callout type="warning" title="Two minus signs in a row">
In $5 - (-2)$, the first "$-$" means **subtract** and the second means **negative**. They are different symbols doing different jobs. Rewrite before you calculate: $5 - (-2) = 5 + 2 = 7$.
</Callout>

## Addition and subtraction together

When an expression has several additions and subtractions, rewrite **every** subtraction as adding the opposite. Then it's just a list of numbers to add, in any order.

## Applications

A **difference** or a **change** is found by subtracting:

- The range between a high and a low value is $\text{high} - \text{low}$.
- The change from an old value to a new value is $\text{new} - \text{old}$. A negative change means it went **down**.

<StepReveal
  client:visible
  title="Elevation difference"
  problem="The highest point in a region is $3133$ m above sea level, and the lowest point is $-28$ m. What is the difference in elevation?"
  steps={[
    { show: '$3133 - (-28)$', why: 'Highest minus lowest.' },
    { show: '$= 3133 + 28 = 3161$', why: 'Add the opposite.' },
  ]}
  answer="The difference is $3161$ m."
/>

## Watch out for these

<Callout type="warning" title="Common mistakes">
1. **Changing only one thing.** $7 - (-3)$ becomes $7 + 3$. You must change the operation **and** the sign of the second number.
2. **Changing the first number.** Only the number being subtracted changes. In $-5 - 2$, the $-5$ stays $-5$: $-5 + (-2) = -7$.
3. **Subtracting in the wrong order.** $3 - 10 \ne 10 - 3$. Subtraction is not commutative.
</Callout>

## Summary

- **$a - b = a + (-b)$:** to subtract, add the opposite.
- Subtracting a **negative** is the same as adding a positive: $a - (-b) = a + b$.
- Rewrite every subtraction as addition, then use the addition rules from Lesson 1.3.
- Ranges and changes are differences: high − low, new − old.
