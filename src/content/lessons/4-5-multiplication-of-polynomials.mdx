## Multiplying monomials

Multiply the coefficients, and multiply the variable parts with the product rule ($a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}$).

$$(-4x^3)(6x^5) = -24x^8 \qquad (3a^2)(-a) = -3a^3$$

<Callout type="warning" title="Count the products">
A binomial times a binomial gives $2 \times 2 = 4$ products before collecting; a binomial times a trinomial gives $6$. If you have fewer, something is missing.
</Callout>

## Summary

- Monomials: multiply coefficients, add exponents.
- Polynomials: every term times every term, then collect like terms.
