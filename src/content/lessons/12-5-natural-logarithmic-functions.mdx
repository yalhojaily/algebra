## Natural logarithms

$$\ln x = \log_e x \qquad \ln e^x = x \qquad e^{\ln x} = x \qquad \ln 1 = 0 \qquad \ln e = 1$$

All the rules from 12.4 hold for $\ln$. Type `ln(x)` and `e^(2x)`.

## Change of base

$$\log_b M = \frac{\ln M}{\ln b} = \frac{\log M}{\log b}$$

## Summary

- $e \approx 2.71828$; $\ln x = \log_e x$.
- $\ln e^x = x$, $e^{\ln x} = x$.
- $\log_b M = \frac{\ln M}{\ln b}$.
